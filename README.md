# LumberWhack!

You finally saved up some money to buy a plot of land and build yourself a home, but the person who sold you the land forgot to mention all the trees (and there's a lot of them).
Guess it's time to get to chopping!

- Either alone or with friends, chop down trees and process them into planks and other goodies to sell!
- Upgrade your stats to make it so you can throw giant trees like its nothing, or chop down multiple trees with one swing!
- Start small, and upgrade your house until you have the mansion of your dreams!
- Automate wood processing and selling to become the worlds most profitable lumberjack!
